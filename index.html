    BOUNDARIES (READ THESE)

    What ColdFact is — and isn't.

    Is this a lie detector?

    No. It's not judge, jury, or polygraph. It scores concreteness, pressure, and vagueness — nothing else.

    Does it analyze the person, or the sample?

    Only the sample. The same speaker can score differently on a different day, a different topic, or a different sixty seconds.

    Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

    Not in this preview. The demo above runs on fictional text — no microphone, no upload, nothing stored.

    Who is this actually for?

    People who read speech for a living and are tired of guessing: coaches, journalists, hiring teams, creators.
